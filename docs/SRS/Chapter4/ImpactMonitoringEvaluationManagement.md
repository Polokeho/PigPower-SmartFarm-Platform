1. Module Overview

The Impact, Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) Management Module will measure, monitor, analyze and report the economic, agricultural, social and environmental outcomes generated by the PigPower platform.

The module is particularly important because PigPower is designed not only as a commercial agribusiness but as a national agricultural transformation platform.

The system must therefore answer questions such as:

How many farmers have joined PigPower?
How many are actively producing?
How much additional income are farmers generating?
How many jobs have been created?
How many young people have been employed?
How many women are participating?
How many pigs are being produced?
How much pork is being processed?
How much imported pork is being substituted?
How much agricultural waste is being converted into energy?
How much renewable energy is being generated?
How much fertilizer is being produced?
What environmental benefits are being achieved?
Is PigPower meeting its BEDCO funding commitments?
Is the business achieving its investor and growth targets?

The module will transform operational data generated throughout the platform into measurable impact indicators.

5. M&E Hierarchy

The system should distinguish between:

Inputs

Resources invested into the programme.

Activities

What PigPower does.

Outputs

Immediate measurable results.

Outcomes

Changes resulting from the outputs.

Impact

Long-term economic, social and environmental effects.

This distinction is important because the system should not incorrectly label an activity as an impact.

The system shall record indicator measurements.

The system shall support configurable impact surveys.

IME-FR-009 — Evidence

The system shall associate supporting evidence with impact records.

The system shall support review and verification of impact data.

IME-FR-011 — Grant Reporting

The system shall track grant-specific objectives and KPIs.

IME-FR-012 — Geographic Analysis

The system shall support impact analysis by geographic area.

IME-FR-013 — Demographic Analysis

The system shall support appropriate demographic disaggregation.

IME-FR-014 — Environmental Metrics

The system shall calculate or record environmental indicators.

IME-FR-015 — Energy Metrics

The system shall retrieve renewable-energy performance indicators.

IME-FR-016 — Economic Metrics

The system shall retrieve farmer and business economic indicators.

IME-FR-017 — Reporting

The system shall generate configurable impact reports.

IME-FR-018 — Dashboard

The system shall provide impact dashboards.

IME-FR-019 — Auditability

The system shall maintain an audit trail for material impact data changes.

IME-FR-020 — Data Quality

The system shall identify the source and verification status of impact data.

54. Non-Functional Requirements
Performance

Impact dashboards should load within an acceptable response time under normal operating conditions.

Availability

Historical impact data should remain accessible even when individual field devices are offline.

Security

Access to sensitive farmer and financial information must be role-controlled.

Scalability

The system must support expansion from the initial pilot to at least the planned 500-farmer network and beyond.

Auditability

Material KPI calculations must be reproducible.

Maintainability

Indicator definitions should be configurable without requiring application-code changes wherever practical.

Interoperability

The module should consume data through APIs from other PigPower modules.
