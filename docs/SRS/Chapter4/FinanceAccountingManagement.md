1. Module Overview

The Finance & Accounting Management Module shall provide the financial management and accounting infrastructure for PigPower Lesotho.

It shall consolidate financial information generated by:

Farm management

The module shall transform operational transactions into financial information that management can use to answer:

How much money is PigPower making, where is it being spent, who owes PigPower, who does PigPower owe, and is each part of the value chain profitable?

PigPower is not simply a farming operation.

It is intended to become an integrated:

production → aggregation → processing → distribution → energy → circular-economy platform.

62. Important Architectural Principle

We should not duplicate financial calculations throughout the application.

For example, Sales should not independently calculate accounting entries.

Instead:

Sales Module
      ↓
Financial Event
      ↓
Accounting Engine
      ↓
General Ledger

Similarly:

Farmer Settlement
      ↓
Financial Event
      ↓
Accounting Engine
      ↓
General Ledger

This will make the system much easier to maintain.
