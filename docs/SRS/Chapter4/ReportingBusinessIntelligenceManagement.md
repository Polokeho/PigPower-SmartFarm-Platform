1. Module Overview

The Reporting & Business Intelligence (BI) Management Module will transform operational, financial, agricultural, commercial, environmental and impact data generated by the PigPower platform into actionable information for decision-making.

The module is the primary information and decision-support layer of the platform.

Where the individual operational modules answer:

"What happened?"

the BI module should help management answer:

"Why did it happen, what is likely to happen next, and what should we do?"

The module will aggregate information from:

User Management
Farmer Management
Farm Management
Pig Management
Production Management
Veterinary Management
Feed Management
Collection & Logistics
Processing & Meat Production
Inventory & Warehouse
Sales & Orders
Customer & CRM
Farmer Payments
Finance & Accounting
Procurement & Suppliers
HR & Payroll
Renewable Energy & Biodigester Management
Impact, Monitoring & Evaluation

The result is a centralized PigPower Business Intelligence Centre.

54. Data Security

The BI layer must not become a mechanism for bypassing operational security.

For example, a user who cannot access a farmer's personal information should not gain access to it simply by generating a report.

The reporting engine must enforce the same authorization model as the underlying platform.

61. Non-Functional Requirements
Performance

Common dashboards should load within an acceptable response time under normal operating conditions.

Scalability

The architecture should support growth from the initial pilot to the 500-farmer target and beyond.

Security

BI access must respect underlying role-based permissions.

Reliability

Critical reports must be reproducible.

Accuracy

The system must clearly distinguish actual, calculated, estimated and forecast values.

Maintainability

KPI formulas and report definitions should be configurable wherever practical.

Auditability

Reports distributed externally should be traceable to the underlying reporting period and data.

Usability

Dashboards should be understandable to users without technical or data-science backgrounds.
